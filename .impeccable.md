## Design Context

### Users
One operator, at a desk, running an outbound calling campaign for a private tax
representation firm. They sit with this open for hours: loading a list, starting the
run, then watching lines go live and reading transcripts as they come in. They are
accountable for what the AI says on their behalf — every call is a compliance event.

### Brand Personality
Warm, mechanical, accountable. This is equipment, not a SaaS dashboard. It should feel
like something with a serial number on the back.

### Aesthetic Direction
A telephone switchboard. Warm charcoal panels, amber signal lamps, oxidized green for a
connected line, brick red for stop. Dark, because the operator dwells here for hours and
the lamps have to read instantly across a glance. Explicitly not: neon-on-black, glass
cards, purple gradients, evenly spaced identical tiles.

Type: Familjen Grotesk (display), Chivo (body/UI), Martian Mono (numbers, timers, phone
numbers only — tabular data, never decoration).

### Design Principles
1. Status is a lamp, not a label. State reads at a glance from across the room.
2. Compliance is visible, never buried. If a number can't be called, the row says why.
3. The transcript is the product. Give it room and let it stream.
4. Destructive and outward-facing actions look different from everything else.
5. Density over whitespace in the roster; air around the live panel.
